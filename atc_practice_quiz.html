Basic ATC practice quiz — hard

100 questions on the Entry Point North Basic ATC syllabus (v16.00), aligned with the EPN training documents for Aircraft, Equipment and Systems, Human Factors, Meteorology and Navigation. Each question is tagged with its syllabus reference and level. Wrong options are built from common misconceptions, and option length gives nothing away.

Level 1 recall a fact or definition. Level 2 explain, describe or differentiate. Level 3 apply: calculate, decode, choose. Every question has four options and exactly one correct answer. Answer order is always shuffled.
